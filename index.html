Our Story

Why Gun Gobar?

Gun Gobar was born from a simple belief — that what is considered waste can be transformed into something sacred and beautiful. We work with rural craftswomen and farmers to create products that honour India's ancient wisdom of panchgavya.

Every product you buy supports sustainable livelihoods, reduces chemical pollution, and brings the warmth of tradition into your home.
